# TillPOS 1.1 – Failures never hold the queue, 2-decimal money, newest scan on top

Date: 2026-10-16 · Status: draft for owner review · Applies to: TillPOS 1.0.1 → 1.1.0 (both production tills)

## Why

Between 9 and 16 October 2026 the production tills built up queues of up to ~2,800 bills. Each time, **one** bill that ERPNext refused (precision changed to 2, item disabled after the sale, below-cost guard, …) stopped everything behind it:

1. a shift's **POS Closing Shift** is sent only when **every** bill of the shift is in ERPNext;
2. ERPNext / POS Awesome allow **one open shift per user and POS Profile**, so the next **POS Opening Shift** waits for that closing;
3. every bill of the later shifts waits for its opening.

Separately, the owner wants **money in 2 decimals** everywhere (ERPNext was switched to 2 twice by a back-office user, which made weighed-item bills fail), and the sale screen must **show the latest scanned item** without scrolling.

## Goals

1. A bill that ERPNext refuses goes to the **Failed** tab and **never holds** other bills, its shift's closing, or later shifts. It can be fixed and retried at any time.
2. The shift's report and cash reconciliation in ERPNext always reflect **every paid bill**, whether or not each invoice has uploaded.
3. Money is calculated and shown with **2 decimals**, matching ERPNext exactly. Weights keep **3 decimals** (grams).
4. The **latest scanned item is the top line** of the bill on the sale screen, highlighted.

Unchanged: shift opening and closing at the till (blind count, supervisor PIN, Z report), one shift at a time per counter, the order opening → bills → closing, and offline selling.

## 1. A failed bill never holds the queue

### Rules

| Bill state in the shift | Closing waits for it? | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Synced | – | already in ERPNext |
| Pending (not tried yet, or its retry time not reached) | **yes** | it is tried first; if ERPNext refuses it, it becomes Failed and the closing goes in the same run |
| In flight / unknown outcome (sent, no answer yet) | **yes** | it may already be in ERPNext under this shift; avoids a duplicate or an orphan |
| **Failed** (ERPNext refused it, or totals differ, or escalated after 3 unknown answers) | **no** | parked; listed in Failed |
| Excluded / Handled | no | as today |

- When ERPNext **cannot be reached** (network, timeout, 5xx, authentication), the run stops and starts over, exactly as today: this is not about one document.
- A **return** still waits for its own original sale (ERPNext needs `return_against`). Only that return waits.

### The shift closing

`ClosingShiftPayload` keeps sending the till's own figures:

- `grand_total`, `net_total` and `payment_reconciliation` (opening, expected, counted, difference per mode) come from the till's closing, **so they include parked bills**. This is already the case.
- **Change:** the VAT row (`taxes`) is built from the shift's **full** VAT (`closing`/all receipts of the shift), not only from the uploaded invoices, so the closing is internally consistent.
- `pos_transactions` lists only the invoices that are in ERPNext (they must exist to be linked).
- `total_quantity`: all bills of the shift (till's figure).

To verify on dev before release: POS Awesome keeps these submitted totals and does not recompute them from `pos_transactions` when a bill is missing.

### Sending a parked bill later (Retry)

1. If its original shift is **still open** in ERPNext: sent as today, linked to that shift.
2. If its original shift is **already closed** in ERPNext: sent linked to the **counter's current open ERPNext shift** of this till (`posa_pos_opening_shift`), keeping its **original posting date and time**, payments and lines. It is then included in that shift's closing list.
3. If the counter has **no open shift** in ERPNext yet (till between shifts): it waits with the note *"Waits for the next shift of <counter> to open in ERPNext"*, and goes once one opens.
4. **A draft left in ERPNext** by an earlier refused submit (e.g. "Partial Payment…", totals differ): on Retry the till **rewrites that draft** from its own bill (lines, payments, taxes, shift link) and then submits it, so nobody has to delete drafts by hand. A draft that was not created by this till (different `posa_client_request_id`) is never touched.

Accounting effect (documented for the owner): until a parked bill is sent, ERPNext's invoices for its old shift are short by that bill, while the old shift's closing already shows the paid amounts and cash count. When it is sent, its sales, VAT and stock post once, on its original date, through the newer shift's consolidation.

### Upload problems / Sync status

- A parked bill's row says: *"Shift <client id> closed without this bill — Retry sends it with the current shift."*
- Failed, Retry, Mark as handled, View: unchanged.

### Tests (xUnit with FakeErp)

- A refused bill in shift 1 → shift 1 closing is sent; shift 2 opening and bills are sent; the refused bill stays Failed.
- An in-flight / unknown bill → closing waits (no change from today).
- Retry after shift 1 closed in ERPNext → bill is sent with shift 2's ERPNext name, original posting date/time kept.
- Retry with no open shift → waits with the note; sent after the next opening.
- Retry when a draft exists for its client id → draft is updated, then submitted; no second insert.
- A return of a parked sale waits; other returns do not.
- ERPNext unreachable → run stops, nothing marked Failed (unchanged).
- Closing payload VAT = full shift VAT including the parked bill.

## 2. 2-decimal money, 3-decimal weights

### ERPNext (production), set by the owner at the switch-over

- System Settings → **Currency Precision = 2**, **Float Precision = 3**.
- Back-office users must not change these while the tills are live (the till and ERPNext must agree).

### Till

- The till's `Precision` (money only: prices, line amounts, VAT, totals, cash and card due, shift figures) changes from **3 to 2**. Weights from scale labels are not rounded by it and keep 3 decimals.
- 1.1.0 adds **Money decimals (2 or 3)** to the supervisor **Settings** screen, saved in `settings.json`.
  - It can only be changed when **no shift is open**, so all bills of a shift use the same rounding.
  - The change is logged as a TillPOS Approval (Settings change).
- The receipt and screen already print money with 2 decimals; no change there.

### Proof on dev before production

1. Dev ERPNext: Currency 2, Float 3.
2. A scratch till (developer key) at money decimals 2: sell a mix of bills — packed items, weighed items (several weights), VAT, pricing rules, cash with 0.25 rounding up and down, card exact, split, returns — and upload Live.
3. Every bill must submit with no totals difference; `SyncCli replay` with Precision 2 on recent dev invoices must report no mismatch.

### Switch-over on production

After both tills show Waiting = 0, at a shift change with no shift open on either till: owner sets ERPNext Currency 2 / Float 3 → supervisor sets Money decimals = 2 on each till → open the next shift. Bills sold before the switch keep their 3-decimal totals; with section 1 in place, any of them refused later goes to Failed without holding the queue.

## 3. Newest scanned item on top

- The bill list on the sale screen shows lines **newest first**: the line just scanned or added (scan, search, F3 quantity change, weighed label) is at the **top and selected/highlighted**.
- Scanning an item already on the bill increases that line's quantity and **moves it to the top**.
- Only the screen order changes. The cart, the printed receipt, the saved bill and the ERPNext invoice keep the order items were added (oldest first).
- Held/recalled bills and the autosaved bill keep working; Del removes the selected (top) line as today.
- Tests: SaleViewModel — after each add the first line is the added one and it is selected; re-scan moves the line to the top; receipt line order unchanged.

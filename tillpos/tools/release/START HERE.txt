TILLPOS {{VERSION}} - TILL {{TILL}} (PRODUCTION)
=======================================
This TillPOS.exe is for TILL {{TILL}} only. It has Till {{TILL}}'s own ERPNext login built
in and connects to the shop's real ERPNext (https://erp.quickgroc.com).
Keep it on this till PC only; do not copy it to other PCs or share it.

BEFORE THE FIRST START (once per PC)
1. The PC's clock and time zone: "(UTC+04:00) Abu Dhabi, Muscat", the time
   set correctly. Receipts print the PC's time.
2. If this PC ran a TEST version of TillPOS before, its test bills are in
   C:\ProgramData\TillPOS. Close TillPOS and rename that folder to
   C:\ProgramData\TillPOS-old-test (do not delete it), so this version
   starts clean. (C:\ProgramData\TillPOS-Dev belongs to the DEV test version
   and is never used by this one.)
3. Connect the receipt printer (installed in Windows) and the cash drawer
   (drawer cable into the printer's DK port). Internet is needed for the
   first start.

FIRST START
1. Copy TillPOS.exe to the Desktop and double-click it. Windows may say
   "Windows protected your PC": click "More info", then "Run anyway".
2. The setup screen opens: pick the receipt printer, press "Test print"
   (a slip comes out, the drawer opens), keep Till number {{TILL}}, press
   "Save and start".
3. The till downloads the items, prices, counters and cashiers from
   ERPNext. The cashiers log in with their PINs from ERPNext's
   "POS Cashier" list.

SWITCH UPLOADS ON - BEFORE THE FIRST REAL SHIFT
Uploads to ERPNext are OFF in this package until the shop decides to go
live. On go-live day, BEFORE opening the first shift:
1. On the Login screen press "Settings" and enter a supervisor's PIN.
2. Under "Upload to ERPNext" choose "Live", then save.
3. The till restarts; the top bar shows a red "LIVE UPLOAD" badge.
From then on every bill, return, shift and approval goes to ERPNext
within about 10 seconds (or as soon as the internet is back).
Bills made while uploads were Off are NOT uploaded, so do not sell
real bills before switching to Live.

COUNTERS
When opening a shift choose the counter: "{{COUNTER}}" is this till's
own counter (it comes first); "{{OTHER_COUNTER}}" can be chosen when
the till serves the other counter. Count the drawer and open the shift.
Only one system per counter at a time: do not run POS Awesome on a
counter while TillPOS has a shift open on it.

EVERY DAY
- Log in with your PIN. Scan items; F12 cash (rounded to 0.25),
  F11 card (exact amount), Split for card + cash.
- F9 Delivery: prints a NOT PAID delivery invoice; take the payment later
  from "Deliveries" (or scan the slip).
- Returns (F6), Hold/Recall (F5/F7), Void (F8, supervisor), Reprint (Ctrl+P).
- Log out keeps the shift open for the next cashier.
- Close shift: count the cash and type the card machine total; a
  supervisor enters their PIN; the Z report prints.
- The top bar shows "synced", "uploaded today", "waiting" and "failed".
  Click it to see details. "failed" needs a supervisor (Upload problems).

PRICE CHANGES (weighed items too)
Change the Item Price in ERPNext. The till picks it up within about
15 seconds, or at once with "Update prices (F10)" on the sale screen.
Items already on an open bill take the new price (the screen says
"Price updated: ..."); a bill on the payment screen, deliveries and
finished bills keep their price.

NEW CASHIERS / PIN CHANGES
Done in ERPNext (POS Cashier list). The till picks them up within about
90 seconds; no restart needed.

IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
Send the file C:\ProgramData\TillPOS\errors.log (if it exists), a photo
of the screen, and the version shown on the Login screen ({{VERSION}}).

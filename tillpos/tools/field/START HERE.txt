FIRST-TIME SETUP
The setup screen opens by itself the first time.
1. Pick your receipt printer from the list.
2. Press "Test print". A test slip should come out.
3. Check that the cash drawer opens.
4. Press "Save and start".
If the test print fails, check that the printer is on and try again. To start
without a printer, choose "(No printer - save receipts as files)" in the list.
#DEV#If more than one PC tests at the same time, give each PC its own till

COUNTERS
When you open a shift, first press the counter you are working at, then
count the cash in its drawer. The counter stays with the shift until the
shift is closed; the top bar shows it next to the till number.
A counter marked "Not available" cannot be used on this till: its settings
could not be downloaded from ERPNext (for example the test login cannot
read it), or it uses a different price list or company. Choose another one.
At "Test Counter" cash is not rounded to 0.25 (its ERPNext profile turns
rounding off), so amounts can show three decimals, for example 9.875.
At "Counter 1" cash is rounded to 0.25 as usual.
#DEV#"Counter 2" is the DEV ERPNext's "Al Ain Counter 2".
The counters are set up on the setup screen (Settings on the Login screen).

KEYS
F2      Search for an item
F3      Change quantity
F11     Pay by card
F12     Pay by cash
Ctrl+P  Reprint the last bill or credit note (marked COPY); it is also
        shown on screen, so you can see it worked even without a printer
Delete  Remove a line (needs the supervisor PIN)
Enter   Confirm
Esc     Cancel / go back

PAYING BY CARD
The Card screen shows the exact amount to charge on the card machine (no
cash box and no change). Charge that amount, then press "Complete & print".
For card + cash, choose "Split".

BARCODE SCANNER
The scanner must press Enter after the code. Most USB scanners do this
already. If a scan does not add the item, check this setting.

CASH DRAWER
Connect the drawer cable to the DK (RJ11) port on the back of the receipt
printer. The drawer opens on cash sales only.

QR CODE ON THE RECEIPT
The receipt ends with a SAMPLE QR code, with "SAMPLE QR - FOR TESTING ONLY"
under it. It is only there to check that the printer prints QR codes well
(size, sharpness, position). It is NOT a valid tax QR code. Please check
that a phone camera can read it.

NO PRINTER CHOSEN?
Receipts are saved as files in {{DATA_FOLDER}}\receipts
(the QR code shows there as "[QR code]").

CHANGING THE PRINTER LATER
On the Login screen press "Settings", enter the supervisor PIN, and choose
the new printer.

SYNC STATUS
The top bar shows the connection and the uploads, for example
"Online · synced 09:25 · 12 uploaded today · 0 waiting · 0 failed".
Click it (on any screen) to open the Sync status window: what the last
download from ERPNext did (and what a "sync problem" was, in plain words),
what was uploaded today, what is waiting and why, and what failed. Anyone
can look; no PIN is needed. "Open Upload problems (supervisor)" there opens
the Upload problems screen after the supervisor PIN.
